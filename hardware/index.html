    JP150PCB 1.3PCB 1.42026-11-04Jan PřibyslavskýThis page does not apply to the product or PCB revision chosen in the header.

    Download the PDF datasheetJP150 Datasheet & User Guide, revision 0.6 · 8.8 MB

    This section describes the board itself: the specifications, every connector with its pinout, the indicators, the configuration, the power levels and the mechanical data, for PCB revision 1.3 and 1.4.

    • Specifications

      Electrical and audio specifications, absolute maximum ratings, environmental limits.

    • Connectors and pinouts

      Every connector of both revisions with its pinout, and the pin-order changes of PCB 1.4.

    • Indicators and LEDs

      The power, status and DSP LEDs, the level meter, the special patterns and the extra LEDs of PCB 1.4.

    • Configuration

      Bridging the power stages, the DIP switch of PCB 1.3, the jumpers and settings of PCB 1.4.

    • Power levels

      The five power levels, what each switches off, and the idle currents.

    • Mechanical

      Board outline, mounting holes and height.

    • Safety

      Supply, heat, speaker wiring and handling precautions.
