    JP150 documentation¶

    Last update 2026-11-05Jan PřibyslavskýReport a problem

    Everything about the JP150 amplifier module in one place: hardware, operation, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, the DSP, firmware updates, MuraLink and the control interface. The pages describe the current firmware, 26.10.1. Where PCB revision 1.3 and 1.4 differ, a page shows both, side by side.
